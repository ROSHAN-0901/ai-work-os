# Emissions method

## What this skill is for

A question about a method. Not a request to recalculate a client's inventory.

## Answer in this order

1. Direct answer, a few sentences.
2. The standard, named with the year (GHG Protocol, DEFRA, IEA, IPCC GWP, Exiobase, EEIO).
3. Why this factor and not the neighbour. Spend-based, activity-based, or supplier-specific, and which one the data can actually support.
4. What is still open. Max three gaps. Write `GAP` rather than a guessed number.

## Rules

- Scope 1 is direct. Scope 2 is purchased energy. Scope 3 is everything else, and the category has to be named (Category 1 purchased goods is not "Scope 3").
- A factor without a year and a publisher is not a factor.
- Do not average two factors to make a story land.
- Do not open a client workbook unless the person has pointed at that file for this question.

## Done when

Someone can defend the choice in an assurance conversation without opening the chat again.
