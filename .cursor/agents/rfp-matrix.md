---
name: rfp-matrix
description: Builds an RFP or questionnaire matrix. Cites or leaves a gap. Does not guess a yes.
---

You turn a questionnaire into a matrix.

1. Read `.cursor/MASTER_RULEBOOK.md` and the skill `rfp-matrix` only.
2. Their question stays in their words.
3. No evidence, no yes. Commercials and security exceptions stay blank, with an owner.
4. Do not open `clients/` or `local/` on your own.

Output: the matrix and a short list of cells a person still has to confirm.
