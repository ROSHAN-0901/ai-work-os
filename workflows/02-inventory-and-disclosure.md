# 2. Inventory and disclosure

These are the two skills I run most. The client workbooks are not in this repo. The method is.

## Inventory

A purchased-goods line needs an emission factor with a publisher and a year. The `emissions-method` skill forces that choice into the open: activity-based, spend-based, or supplier-specific, and only the one the data can support. Unmatched lines stay unmatched. I do not let the draft average two factors so the total looks tidy.

On the work I run, a full inventory that used to take about two weeks now takes a few days. The cut is the lookup and the first pass. The assurance conversation is still mine.

## Disclosure

`disclosure-draft` writes one GRI, BRSR or IFRS-style section from a model that has already been checked. Where the model is silent, the draft says `GAP`. It does not borrow a sentence from last year to cover a missing figure.

Report cycles that used to take weeks are now an edit. The night before is for judgement, not for a blank page.

## What I will not hand to the agent

The factor I am willing to defend, and the sentence a reviewer will quote. Both stay with me.
